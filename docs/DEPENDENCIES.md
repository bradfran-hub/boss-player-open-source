# Dependency requirements for Free and paid BOSS

This is a retained-source inventory, not a claim that every candidate framework is linked into every final product. The Free edition has a separate build graph. Each final Free and paid artefact must record its actual dependency graph, source/build hashes, notices, applicable licences and the evidence closing its obligations.

## Completion rule

An edition is not marked covered until all dependencies actually included in that edition have an identified licence, complete required notices/source, a pinned mapping to the final artefact and any required relinking/replacement/installation materials. Unknown ownership, unknown binary provenance and unresolved GPL/commercial conflicts remain open. These gaps are not automatically proof of a violation, but prevent claiming the audit is complete.

The dependency materials are freely accessible irrespective of whether a recipient purchased BOSS. The repository does not charge for exercising GPL/LGPL rights.
